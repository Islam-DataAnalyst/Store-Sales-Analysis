# 🚀 Store Sales Analysis
### Retail Business Intelligence Project | DEPI Graduation 2026

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Retail companies generate massive amounts of transactional data every day. However, many organizations struggle to convert this data into meaningful business insights.

The **Store Sales Analysis** project analyzes a Superstore dataset containing **9,800 transactions** across multiple regions, product categories, and customer segments. The objective is to transform raw retail data into actionable insights that can help businesses:

* **Increase revenue** through strategic product focus.
* **Reduce operational inefficiencies**.
* **Improve regional sales performance**.
* **Enable data-driven decision making**.

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset contains **9,800 records** and **18 variables**, representing different dimensions of the retail business.

### 🔹 Key Dimensions
* **Order ID** & **Ship Mode**.
* **Customer Segment**.
* **Geographic Data:** Region, State, and City.
* **Product Details:** Category and Sub-Category.

### 🔹 Key Metrics
* **Sales Revenue**.
* **Transaction Volume**.

> This dataset enables multi-dimensional analysis of customer behavior, regional performance, and product demand.

---

## 💼 Business Problems Addressed
This project focuses on solving real business challenges rather than simply visualizing data:

1. **Sales Visibility Gap:** Identifying which products or regions drive the majority of revenue.
2. **Inventory Inefficiency:** Spotting underperforming products to optimize stock.
3. **Regional Performance Imbalance:** Evaluating sales performance by region to guide expansion strategies.
4. **Customer Value Identification:** Segmenting customers to identify high-value groups.

## ⚙️ Analytical Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning:** Handling missing values and validating data consistency.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Discovering patterns in categories, regions, and segments.
3. **Insight Generation:** Transforming findings into business recommendations.
4. **Visualization:** Creating interactive dashboards for stakeholders.

---

## 💡 Key Insights (Initial Findings)
* 📱 **Technology** is the highest revenue-generating category.
* 🗺️ **West Region** leads in overall sales performance.
* 🚚 **Standard Class** is the most frequently used shipping method.
